Dear colleagues,

For the first time, DCG is fielding its own team at PaceDay, and I would love to see as many faculty and staff as possible at the starting line. This year is especially meaningful for our college, as we walk in memory of a dear colleague, Dr. Nancy Young.

There are two ways to take part, and both matter.

Option 1
Run or walk the 5K
You get
›A PaceDay t-shirt
›Food, drinks, music, and lawn games at the finish
›A morning with your DCG colleagues
You commit
›To raise $200 by December 6. Share your page with friends and family, or donate it yourself. Reach $200 and you pay nothing else.
›Registration is free through Sunday, $25 after September 21
Join the team
  
Option 2
Donate to the team
›Any amount, no registration, no minimum
›Give to the team or to a specific teammate
›Tax deductible, and 100% goes to research

Donations to the team page count toward our $1,000 team goal. To support one person, open the roster on the team page and click their name.

Donate
Where and when
DateSunday, October 4, 2026
WhereAU Health Sciences Lawn, 1482 Laney Walker Blvd, Augusta
Check-in7:00 a.m.
Start8:30 a.m.
Distance5K (3.1 miles), walk or run at your own pace
ParkingOn site. Start and finish are at the same place this year.

Kids ages 3 to 13 can join as Youth Walkers for $25 with no fundraising. Family and friends are welcome. Out of town that day? Sign up as a PACELINE Pal and fundraise with us from anywhere.

Where the money goes
100% of every dollar funds cancer research at the Georgia Cancer Center.

Since 2019, PACELINE participants have raised over $2 million, and six of the projects it funded have gone on to earn $12.25 million more from the NIH.
